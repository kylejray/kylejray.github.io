# About Me
Hi, my name is Kyle Ray. I am a postdoc at UC Davis, working under [James Crutchfield](http://csc.ucdavis.edu/~chaos/) at the [complexity science center](http://csc.ucdavis.edu/Welcome.html). 

Whil I specialize in simulation and data analysis, I strive for a holistic, collaborative approach to problem solving. Beyond my research, I am an amateur musician and a very amateur birder. I also enjoy playing almost any kind of video game (though I have a special soft spot for 90’s nostalgia-bomb metroid clones).

This site is really my only "online presence", but the main purpose of it is to document interesting or useful coding tricks I pick up while working. Aditionally, it seves as a good platform to share responses that I come up with in my capacity as a mentor to graduante students at the complexity sciences center. I also use it to host animations and images to supplement publications, manuscripts, and research projects.
